Overview

CommentScope ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains what data we access, how we process it, and what we store.

How the Extension Works

CommentScope offers two extraction modes:

Page Mode (Current Page)

When you click "Extract Comments" while viewing an Instagram post, a script reads comments from the visible page, processes them locally in your browser, and exports them to CSV and/or JSON files on your device.

URL Mode (Paste URL)

When you paste an Instagram post URL, the extension makes requests to Instagram's web API from your browser. These requests use your existing Instagram session (via browser cookies) to authenticate — the same way your browser does when you visit instagram.com. Comments are fetched, processed locally, and exported to your device.

In both modes, all processing happens locally. No comment data is sent to any external server.

What Data We Access

Instagram Comments (Processed Locally)

The extension reads: comment text, usernames and profile URLs, timestamps, and reply/thread structure.

Instagram Session Cookies (URL Mode Only)

In URL mode, the extension reads your Instagram csrftoken cookie to authenticate API requests. This is functionally identical to your browser visiting instagram.com.

We do NOT:

  • Store, copy, or transmit your session cookies
  • Access your Instagram password
  • Access your private messages, stories, or DMs
  • Post, like, comment, follow, or take any write action on your behalf
  • Send cookies or session data to any external server

None. We collect no analytics, no usage data, no personal information. We don't even know how many people use the extension, which posts you extract comments from, or how often you use it.

Third-Party Services

None. The extension does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or external APIs. The only network requests are to instagram.com — using your own browser session.

Data Sharing

We don't share data because we don't collect data. The only "data sharing" that occurs is you downloading the CSV/JSON files created locally. The files stay on your device. You control where they go.

Data Security

Since all processing happens locally in your browser, your data never leaves your device (unless you manually share the exported files). There are no server-side vulnerabilities and no third-party data breaches possible.
